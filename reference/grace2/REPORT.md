# GRACE 2.0: verification report

Generated by reference/grace2/report.py. Function: `cvd_risk.grace2` (1- and 3-year death, and death or MI, after admission for ACS; full and substituted models).

## 1. Sources

- **Developers' calculator (evidence A).** The GRACE 2.0 web app of the Center for Outcomes Research, UMass (https://www.outcomes-umassmed.org/grace/acs_risk2/), from the Wayback Machine. `source/grace_lookups.js`, `grace.js` and `tests.js` (QUnit) are byte-identical in the 2021 and 2024-07-24 captures; `index.html` and `commonFunctions.js` are from the 2024-07-24 capture (20240724120620). The files are unmodified.
- **Paper (evidence C).** Fox KAA, FitzGerald G, Puymirat E, et al. BMJ Open 2014;4:e004425 (`source/fox2014.pdf`). It prints hazard ratios only (Table 2), no coefficients, knots or baseline survival.
- The coordinating centre's *Methods and formulas used to calculate the GRACE Risk Scores* (2014-01-30, cited by the paper as the source of the model estimates) covers only the GRACE 1.0 models (Granger 2003, Eagle 2004, Fox 2006); it is not stored here.

## 2. What the calculator computes

`commonFunctions.js` calls only `grace_lookups.js`. In-hospital and 6-month results are the GRACE 1.0 models (Granger logistic model; the in-hospital death/MI, Fox 2006 admission-to-6-month death and death/MI point nomograms) and are not part of `grace2`. The 1- and 3-year results are eight Cox models, risk = 1 - S0^exp(sum of terms), each term read from a table at the dropdown entry chosen: every year of age from 50 to 89 (one value each for <30, 30-39, 40-44, 45-49, 90-94 and >=95), and one value per band of heart rate, SBP and creatinine (e.g. heart rate 50-69 is computed at 59.5). If creatinine or Killip class is 'not available' it uses the substituted models (renal insufficiency, diuretics). `grace.js`, loaded by the page only for its QUnit tests, codes the two 1-year death models with continuous inputs.

## 3. Coefficients

The 1-year death models' knots and coefficients are those of `grace.js` (age per 10 years, knots 4.8165/6.6571/8.3095; heart rate per 30 bpm, 1.71/2.31/2.77/3.95; SBP per -20 mmHg, -9.02/-6.97/-5.32; creatinine, 0.685/0.925/1.145/2.065 mg/dL). For the other six models the table values were fitted with the same knots and spline terms; the coefficients come out at 5 decimals, as in `grace.js`, and the baseline survivals are those in `grace_lookups.js`. Every table value (836: 8 models x 72 ages, 10 heart rates, 9 SBPs, 7 creatinines in the full models, Killip or renal + diuretics, 3 yes/no items) is reproduced to the digits printed (`test_coefficients_reproduce_every_calculator_lookup_value`). The exception is the SBP term of the two 3-year death models: no restricted cubic spline with 3 or 4 knots (positions searched over 30-260 mmHg), polynomial or linear spline reproduces it (a 5-knot spline has as many parameters as values), so `grace2` uses the calculator's value for each SBP band.

## 5. Against the paper

Fox 2014 Table 2 prints hazard ratios to one decimal. The 24 categorical and linear terms of the 1-year death, 1-year death/MI and 3-year death models give exactly those values when exp(b) is rounded to two decimals and then to one (22 of 24 when rounded once: 1-year death Killip III exp(0.85325) = 2.347 and IV exp(1.29372) = 3.646 print as 2.4 and 3.7). Table 2 has no 3-year death/MI column. For the 3-year death SBP term Table 2 gives per -20 mmHg HR 0.9 at >=160, 1.6 at 130-159 and 1.3 below 130; the calculator's band values give 0.92 (160-179 to >=180) and 1.58 (130-139 to 140-159), but 1.44 to 2.58 between the bands below 130.

## 6. Findings

1. **The calculator bands its inputs.** The paper says version 2.0 'directly utilises model estimates themselves' instead of 'using intervals for continuous variables such as age, as in V.1.0'. The web calculator evaluates the spline models at one value per band of heart rate, SBP and creatinine, and of age below 50 and from 90. `grace2` takes continuous inputs and equals the calculator at its band values.
2. **3-year death SBP term.** Its values fit no spline and, below 130 mmHg, not the hazard ratio in Table 2 (section 5); `grace2` uses the band values.
3. **`grace.js` fails its own tests.** With the `Number.prototype.roundup` the calculator ships (which returns Math.round(x), a whole number, for x >= 10) 69 of 460 QUnit checks fail, because spline terms of 10 and above are rounded to whole numbers; without that branch 1 fails (`age_toDecades(0)` expected to be 1). The calculator does not use `grace.js`, so its results are not affected.
4. **6-month death in the calculator** (GRACE 1.0, Fox 2006 admission-to-6-month nomogram; not part of `grace2`): the heart-rate value for 150-199 bpm is 34.37 (computed at 174.5), the in-hospital death/MI nomogram's value; the Fox 2006 nomogram in the coordinating centre's formulas gives 22 + (174.5 - 150) x 0.3 = 29.35. Its score-to-probability steps leave scores above 6 and up to 7 without a probability (the result is NaN) and set 113% above 240 points; the result is then multiplied by 80/91 and values above 90% are set to 92%.
5. **Merck Manuals 'GRACE 2.0 Score (non-linear model 2014)'** (https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/multimedia/clinical-calculator/grace-20-score-for-acute-coronary-syndrome-prognosis-non-linear-model-2014) computes the Fox 2006 admission-to-6-month death points (Killip 0/15/29/44, cardiac arrest 30, enzymes 13, ST deviation 17; age, heart rate, SBP and creatinine interpolated within bands), not the GRACE 2.0 Cox models. Its formulas equal the developers' calculator's 6-month death tables at 72/72 Age, 9/10 Pulse, 9/9 BloodPressure, 7/7 Creatine entries; the exception is the heart-rate band of finding 4, where Merck gives 29.35.
6. In the 3-year models positive enzymes have no effect and Killip III and IV share one coefficient (as Table 2 reports).
