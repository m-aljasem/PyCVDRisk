<font size="3">

**TIMIcalculators** is a compendium of risk scores developed at the [Thrombolysis in Myocardial Infarction (TIMI) Study Group](https://timi.org), an academic research organization of Brigham and Women's Hospital and Harvard Medical School.

This package has been developed by [**Giorgio Melloni**](https://gmelloni.github.io/) and [**Andrea Bellavia**](https://andreabellavia.github.io/).

An interactive app with the same functionalities is available here, [**TIMI calculators App**](https://timibiostat.shinyapps.io/calculators/)

</font>

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Functionalities

<font size="3">

This app can currently calculate the following TIMI risk scores:

- *TIMI Risk Score for UA/NSTEMI*
- *TIMI Risk Score for STEMI*
- *TIMI Risk Index for Mortality (STEMI or NSTEMI)*
- *TIMI Risk Score for Secondary Prevention (2°P) in ASCVD*
- *TIMI Risk Score for Heart Failure in Diabetes (TRS-HF<sub>DM</sub>)*
- *TIMI Biomarker Score for Heart Failure in Diabetes (TBS-HF<sub>DM</sub>)*
- *TIMI Risk Score for Atherothrombosis in Diabetes (TRS-MACE<sub>DM</sub>)*
- *TIMI AF score for severe net clinical outcome (TIMI-AF)*
- *TIMI Risk Score for Kidney Disease Progression in Diabetes (TRS-KDP<sub>DM</sub>)*
- *TIMI Risk Score for New Onset of Diabetes (TRS-NODM)*
- *TIMI Risk score for SEE in AF (SEE-AF)*
- *ApoB estimator*

Formula, reference and rationale of each score is provided.
